## Welcome Screen

Users can choose one of the following:

### Sign In
- For existing users.

### Create an Account
- Register a new account.

### Continue as Guest
- Explore public budgets and government information without registering.

### Learn About BudgetWatch Kenya
The platform enables citizens to:
- Track the national budget.
- Monitor government spending.
- Follow public projects.
- Track public debt and loans.
- View audit reports.
- Compare government institutions.
- Participate in public consultations.
