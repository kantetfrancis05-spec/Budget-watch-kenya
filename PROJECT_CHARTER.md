# BudgetWatch Kenya – Project Charter

**Project Name:** BudgetWatch Kenya

**Tagline:** Follow Every Shilling.

**Motto:** Sovereignty Belongs to the People.

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Planning (Phase 0)

---

## 1. Project Vision

To build Kenya's leading civic transparency platform that empowers every citizen to understand, monitor, and participate in the management of public resources through accurate, accessible, and evidence-based information.

---

## 2. Mission Statement

BudgetWatch Kenya aims to improve transparency, accountability, and public participation by providing citizens with a secure digital platform that presents official public finance information in a simple and understandable way.

---

## 3. Project Objectives

The project seeks to:

- Improve public access to government financial information.
- Simplify national and county budget information.
- Track public expenditure using official published data.
- Monitor implementation of publicly funded projects.
- Present procurement and contract information.
- Track Kenya's public debt and major loans.
- Compare institutional performance using transparent and documented methodologies.
- Promote meaningful public participation.
- Support evidence-based civic engagement.
- Encourage accountability through reliable information.

---

## 4. Scope

### Included

- National Budget Dashboard
- County Budget Dashboard
- Government Institution Profiles
- Public Project Tracker
- Procurement Tracker
- National Debt & Loan Tracker
- Audit Report Centre
- Institution Performance Dashboard
- Public Participation Portal
- Citizen Reporting System
- Notifications and Alerts
- AI Civic Assistant
- Analytics and Data Visualizations

### Excluded

The platform will not:

- Access confidential government databases.
- Display classified information.
- Track private bank accounts.
- Display undisclosed personal financial information.
- Make accusations or conclusions unsupported by verified evidence.
- Replace official government records.

---

## 5. Target Users

- Citizens
- Students
- Journalists
- Researchers
- Civil society organizations
- Public institutions
- County governments
- Policy analysts
- Development partners

---

## 6. Core Values

- Transparency
- Accountability
- Accuracy
- Independence
- Security
- Privacy
- Accessibility
- Fairness
- Civic Participation
- Innovation

---

## 7. Guiding Principles

- Every published figure should have a verifiable source.
- Data should be clearly labeled as official, estimated, or community-submitted.
- Users' personal information must be protected.
- Rankings must be based on transparent methodologies.
- The platform should remain politically neutral.
- Accessibility and usability should guide all design decisions.

---

## 8. Expected Outcomes

The completed platform will enable users to:

- Understand national and county budgets.
- Compare spending across institutions.
- Follow public projects.
- Explore procurement information.
- Monitor public debt trends.
- Review audit findings.
- Participate in civic consultations.
- Access financial information from a single platform.

---

## 9. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

- Provides reliable information based on official sources.
- Offers a secure and user-friendly experience.
- Helps citizens better understand public finance.
- Encourages informed public participation.
- Maintains high standards of privacy and data security.
- Can scale to support national usage.

---

## 10. Risks

Potential risks include:

- Changes in data availability.
- Delays in publication of official reports.
- Cybersecurity threats.
- Misinformation and false community reports.
- Infrastructure or hosting limitations.
- Legal and regulatory changes.

Mitigation strategies will be documented in the Security Model and Risk Management documentation.

---

## 11. Future Vision

BudgetWatch Kenya aims to become the country's leading public finance transparency and civic participation platform by providing accurate, accessible, and trustworthy information that empowers citizens while supporting accountability and evidence-based decision-making.

---

## 12. Project Motto

**Sovereignty Belongs to the People.**

BudgetWatch Kenya exists to help citizens understand how public resources are managed and to support informed participation in democratic governance through transparency, accountability, and access to reliable public information.
