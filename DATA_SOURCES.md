# BudgetWatch Kenya – Data Sources

**Version:** 1.0

**Project:** BudgetWatch Kenya

**Tagline:** Follow Every Shilling.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the categories of data used by BudgetWatch Kenya, the expected source of each dataset, how often it should be updated, and how it should be verified before publication.

The platform is committed to using reliable public information and clearly distinguishing between official data, estimated values, and community-submitted content.

---

## 2. Data Source Principles

Every dataset should:

- Come from a reliable source.
- Include the publication date.
- Include the last update date.
- Include the update frequency.
- Include the source organization.
- Include a verification status.
- Be traceable to the original publication.

---

## 3. Data Classification

### Official Data

Published by authorized public institutions.

**Examples:**
- National Budget
- Supplementary Budgets
- County Budgets
- Audit Reports
- Public Debt Reports
- Procurement Notices
- Development Project Reports

**Status Label:** Verified Official Data

---

### Verified Public Data

Published by reputable organizations or official partners.

**Examples:**
- Public datasets released under open-data initiatives.
- Research publications used for contextual analysis.

**Status Label:** Verified Public Data

---

### Estimated Data

Calculated by BudgetWatch Kenya using official published figures.

**Examples:**
- Spending progress estimates.
- Debt repayment projections.
- Budget utilization estimates.

**Status Label:** Estimated

Every estimate should include the methodology used.

---

### Community Data

Submitted by registered users.

**Examples:**
- Photos of projects.
- Project progress reports.
- Citizen observations.
- Public participation comments.

**Status Label:** Community Submitted

Community reports should undergo moderation before public display where appropriate.

---

## 4. Core Data Categories

### National Budget

**Contents:**
- Annual Budget
- Supplementary Budget
- Revenue Estimates
- Expenditure Allocations

**Update Frequency:**
- Annual
- As supplementary budgets are published

**Verification:** Official publication required.

---

### County Budgets

**Contents:**
- County Revenue
- County Expenditure
- Development Budget
- Recurrent Budget

**Update Frequency:** Annual and when official revisions are released.

---

### Government Institutions

**Contents:**
- Institution profile.
- Budget allocation.
- Expenditure.
- Performance indicators.
- Audit observations.

**Update Frequency:** As new official reports become available.

---

### Public Projects

**Contents:**
- Project name.
- Location.
- Budget.
- Contractor.
- Status.
- Funding source.

**Update Frequency:** Whenever new official information is published or verified community updates are accepted.

---

### Procurement

**Contents:**
- Tender notices.
- Awarded contracts.
- Contract values.
- Suppliers.
- Procurement status.

**Update Frequency:** As official procurement information becomes available.

---

### Public Debt

**Contents:**
- Domestic debt.
- External debt.
- Major loans.
- Lenders.
- Repayment information.
- Outstanding balances.

**Update Frequency:** As official debt reports are published.

---

### Audit Reports

**Contents:**
- Audit opinions.
- Findings.
- Recommendations.
- Follow-up actions where publicly available.

**Update Frequency:** Whenever official audit reports are released.

---

### Public Participation

**Contents:**
- Consultation notices.
- Budget participation opportunities.
- Citizen submissions.

**Update Frequency:** As participation opportunities are announced.

---

### Community Reports

**Contents:**
- Project photos.
- Progress updates.
- Citizen observations.
- Local development reports.

**Verification:** Reviewed by moderators before publication where required.

---

## 5. Data Verification Process

Every dataset should pass through the following stages:

1. Collection
2. Source Identification
3. Validation
4. Review
5. Approval
6. Publication
7. Monitoring
8. Update

---

## 6. Metadata Requirements

Every published dataset should include:

- Dataset Name
- Source Organization
- Source Reference
- Publication Date
- Last Updated
- Update Frequency
- Verification Status
- Version Number

---

## 7. Data Quality Standards

The platform aims for data that is:

- Accurate
- Complete
- Consistent
- Timely
- Verifiable
- Relevant

Where information is incomplete or unavailable, this should be clearly stated rather than inferred.

---

## 8. Transparency

Users should be able to:

- View the source of each dataset.
- See when it was last updated.
- Understand whether values are official, estimated, or community-submitted.
- Read the methodology behind rankings and calculated metrics.

---

## 9. Corrections

If errors are identified:

- Record the issue.
- Verify the correction.
- Update the dataset.
- Keep a change history where practical.
- Notify users of significant corrections when appropriate.

---

## 10. Future Expansion

Future versions may incorporate additional publicly available datasets, provided they meet the platform's standards for reliability, legality, and transparency.

**Potential areas include:**

- Environmental expenditure.
- Education performance.
- Health sector statistics.
- Infrastructure indicators.
- Development partner funding.
- Public service delivery metrics.

---

## 11. Data Governance Commitment

BudgetWatch Kenya is committed to publishing information responsibly by:

- Prioritizing official and verifiable sources.
- Clearly labeling estimated and community-generated information.
- Maintaining transparency about methodologies.
- Correcting errors promptly.
- Preserving public trust through consistent data governance.
